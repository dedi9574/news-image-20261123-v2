# News Front Page — 23 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026 in a
single image: **THE WORLD TODAY**.

## The stories

- **Lead — COP31 collapses:** UN climate talks in Antalya end with no deal
  after record overtime; the EU and island states rejected a draft that
  never mentioned fossil fuels.
- **Gulf in flames:** tankers struck in the Strait of Hormuz as the Iran
  war at sea escalates; Iran says two more ships hit naval mines.
- **Warship hit in Libya:** suspected Ukrainian sea drones strike the
  Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi; the ship capsizes, 10 hurt.
- **Kyiv flat hit:** a Russian drone hits a five-storey block in Dymer,
  north of Kyiv: 2 killed and 11 missing, children among them.
- **'El Chapo Isidro' held:** Mexican forces capture FBI most-wanted
  cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores after a gun battle in Sinaloa.
- **Mercury, at last:** after an eight-year journey, the ESA-JAXA
  BepiColombo probe enters orbit around Mercury, only the second ever.
- **Seoul minister quits:** South Korea's education minister resigns over
  chaos in the college entrance exam amid AI-cheating fears.
- **Sport:** India push for victory on day five of the Wellington Test;
  New Zealand 157 for 4 at lunch.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG) to the output directory. No network, no
clock, no randomness — the output is byte-identical on every run.
